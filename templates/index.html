nbidal18 v4.1.3-packwiz

This is the updater-enabled Prism Launcher edition of nbidal18 4.1.2 for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Download the Prism instance

  1. Open Prism Launcher.
  2. Select Add Instance, then Import.
  3. Choose the downloaded ZIP.
  4. Click Play. The first launch downloads the complete pack; later launches download only updates.

Gameplay files stay synchronized. Personal Auto HUD, Voice Chat, Iris, Sodium, keybind, and controller settings are installed with defaults when available and then preserved.

While connected, v4.1.3 clients monitor protected pack files and require a restart if those files change. The server remains in transition mode, so 4.1.1 and 4.1.2 clients can still connect during testing.
